Kite / History

The publisher’s history

The present Kite catalogue traces publishing activity back to 2005. Projects involving Huma and Tuulelohe preceded publications under the Kite imprint.

2005

The first stage was Igor Kotjuh’s poetry collection «Когда наступит завтра?», issued by Huma. Huma published that book; it was not an earlier name for Kite.

2006–2007

Tuulelohe / «Воздушный змей»: the literary almanac and the «Первый полет» series.

2008–2025

The Kite imprint published Estonian- and Russian-language books, poetry, fiction, essays and translations.

2026 →

Kite welcomes new publishing projects and collaborations. Details of earlier publications are still being verified against bibliographic records, correspondence and physical copies.
